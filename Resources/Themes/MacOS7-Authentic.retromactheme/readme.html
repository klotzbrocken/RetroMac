Macintosh System 7.1

Apple · 1992

System 7.1 (1992) was the Mac as most people met it in the early nineties: aliases, a multitasking Finder, TrueType, drag and drop between programs — and on a colour Mac, 256 colours: the rainbow apple, lavender folders and icons drawn from Apple's standard colour table. The theme adds the Control Strip as System 7.5 showed it on a colour Mac, the Application menu at the right end of the menu bar and the Apple menu of the day. There is no dock; programs come from the Finder and the menus.

What made it special

  • The Control Strip: AppleTalk, File Sharing, colour depth, resolution and sound on the screen edge; it rolls in and out under its tab.
  • 256 colours: every icon in the Mac's 8-bit colour table, with the hard edge of a 1-bit mask, and lavender-grey window frames.
  • Aliases, a multitasking Finder, TrueType, and drag and drop between programs — the desktop metaphor grown up.

ⓘ  Did you know…

The Control Strip came with the PowerBook. It shipped in 1994 with the PowerBook 500 series on System 7.1.1, and reached every Mac with System 7.5.
Chicago everywhere. The bold Chicago typeface from the menus later showed up on the original iPod's screen.
