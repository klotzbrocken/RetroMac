Reference: kde1-kdebase at `78afecb4` (10 July 1998), the release of 12 July 1998. Sizes and colours: kpanelrc, kdisplayrc, kpanel.C, tools.C, layout.C, kwm main.C and options.h, kdelibs kapp.cpp. The KDE 1.0 icons are under KDE's licences of the time (GPL/LGPL, see the package COPYING and AUTHORS); some XPM headers name their artist, e.g. Mark Donohoe.
