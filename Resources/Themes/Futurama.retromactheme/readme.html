Futurama

Screen & Sci-Fi · 1999

Futurama is RetroMac's tribute theme — it reimagines your Mac as a cheerful desktop from the world of tomorrow. Soft rounded chrome, a glowing dock and a set of apps renamed for the year 3000 turn the everyday desktop into a friendly piece of retro-future kit.

It's a nod to the animated sci-fi comedy that first aired in 1999 (created by Matt Groening and David X. Cohen) and imagined life a thousand years from now — optimistic, ridiculous and full of blinking machines.

What's in the theme

  • Rounded, glowing window chrome and a soft teal desktop.
  • A dock of world-of-tomorrow apps: OmniBrowser, ZapMail, SqueakiTunes, ChronoCalendar.
  • The Bender-O-Meter and Noted-O-Matic desk accessories.
  • A robot-friendly icon set drawn just for this theme.

ⓘ  Did you know…

The year 3000. The show is set a full millennium in the future, so a "retro" future desktop is really a very, very forward-dated one.
Smart writers' room. The series is famous for a writing staff stacked with maths and science degrees, sneaking real theorems into the background gags.
Cancelled, then not. After its original run it came back more than once — proof that good ideas, like good desktops, are hard to switch off.
