QNX 6.2.1 — Photon

QNX Software Systems · 2003

QNX Neutrino is the realtime operating system inside cars, routers, medical devices and nuclear plants. Version 6.2.1 came on a free non-commercial CD in 2003, and it booted straight into a full graphical desktop: Photon.

At the bottom ran a taskbar with a Launch button, one entry per window and the clock. Down the right edge stood the shelf: groups of launchers you could fold open and shut, a live system monitor, a CD player and World View, a map of nine workspaces.

In RetroMac

  • The shelf opens real Mac apps and settings: Voyager is your web browser, Editor TextEdit, Configure the matching System Settings.
  • The System Monitor shows your Mac's real CPU and memory load.
  • World View opens Mission Control. RetroMac does not fake nine workspaces.
  • Launch lists your apps in Photon's categories. "End Photon session" turns the theme off.

Did you know?

QNX once fit a whole graphical OS with a web browser on a single 1.44 MB floppy disk, as a demo.

Photon's title bars came in colour schemes; the blue one here was the default.
