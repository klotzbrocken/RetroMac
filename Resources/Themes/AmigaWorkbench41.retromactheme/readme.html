Amiga Workbench 4.1

Hyperion, 2008 · AmigaOne · Workbench since 1985

The Amiga of 1985 multitasked pre-emptively, with custom chips for graphics and four-voice sound; Workbench was its desktop, folders were “drawers”. AmigaOS 4.1 (2008) carried it onto PowerPC.

This theme

  • After AmigaOS 4.1 on the AmigaOne, 2008. Everything is redrawn for RetroMac; the backdrop is a reconstruction (its unhidden original is not to hand).
  • Hold the right mouse button for the Workbench menus; Tools has the backdrops (WBPattern), new shortcuts and the Clock, Calculator, Notepad and CPU Monitor. The gadget at the right of the screen bar puts the other programs away and brings them back.
  • RAM Disk lives in your own temporary folder: it outlasts RetroMac, the Mac's restart clears it, as with RAM:.
  • The Mac's apps take their 2008 Amiga counterparts on the AmiDock where there was one (Safari is IBrowse, Mail YAM, Music PlayCD, QuickTime DvPlayer, Terminal Shell, Preview MultiView); the rest keep modern pictures.
  • Drawers show objects with an icon; Window ▸ Show ▸ All files shows the rest. Snapshot keeps places in RetroMac's settings, never in your folders.
