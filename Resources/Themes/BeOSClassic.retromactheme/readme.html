BeOS

Be Inc. · 1995–2001

Be Inc. was founded in 1990 by Jean-Louis Gassée, a former Apple executive, to build a “media OS” from scratch with no legacy baggage. BeOS was pervasively multithreaded, symmetric-multiprocessing from the start, and sat on the 64-bit journaling BeFS whose files you could query like a database.

Its signature look: the yellow window tab you could slide along the title bar, and the corner Deskbar. It first ran on the twin-CPU BeBox before moving to PowerPC and x86.

What made it special

  • Built for media — multithreading everywhere kept audio and video smooth.
  • BeFS: a 64-bit journaling file system with live, database-style queries.
  • The sliding yellow tab and corner Deskbar — recreated by this theme.
  • Lives on today as the open-source Haiku.

ⓘ  Did you know…

Apple almost bought Be. In 1996 Apple shopped for a modern OS foundation and seriously considered BeOS — then chose NeXT instead, which brought Steve Jobs back to Apple.
Blinkenlights. The dual-CPU BeBox had two columns of LEDs on its front bezel that danced with processor load, plus a hobbyist “GeekPort”.
Query your files. BeFS treated file attributes like a database, so a folder could be a live search result.
